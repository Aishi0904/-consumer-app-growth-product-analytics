# Project Methodology

## Stage 1: Data Exploration
Explore the GA4 public ecommerce dataset.

## Stage 2: Data Preparation
Clean and transform event-level data using SQL.

## Stage 3: KPI Development
Calculate business and product performance metrics.

## Stage 4: Analytical Modelling
Prepare datasets and relationships for Power BI.

## Stage 5: Visualisation
Develop interactive dashboards using Power BI and DAX.

## Stage 6: Business Interpretation
Identify patterns, conversion bottlenecks
and retention opportunities.

## Stage 7: Recommendations
Translate findings into actionable
business recommendations.
