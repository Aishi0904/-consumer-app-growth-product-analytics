# Business Problem

## Objective

Analyse consumer digital-product behaviour
to identify opportunities for improving
acquisition, engagement, conversion and retention.

## Key Business Questions

1. Which acquisition sources attract users?
2. How actively do users engage?
3. Where do users drop out of the funnel?
4. Which products generate the most revenue?
5. Are users returning to the platform?
6. What opportunities exist for business growth?

## Expected Outcome

Develop actionable recommendations through
SQL-based analysis and interactive Power BI
dashboards.
