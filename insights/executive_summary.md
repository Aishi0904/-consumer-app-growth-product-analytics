
## Preliminary Purchase Funnel Analysis

### 1. Funnel Metrics

The analysis of the GA4 ecommerce dataset for
November 2020 to January 2021 identified the
following user activity:

| Funnel Stage | Unique Users |
|---|---:|
| Product Viewers | 61,252 |
| Add-to-Cart Users | 12,545 |
| Checkout Users | 9,715 |
| Purchasing Users | 4,419 |

### 2. Conversion KPIs

| KPI | Conversion Rate |
|---|---:|
| View-to-Cart Rate | 20.48% |
| Cart-to-Checkout Rate | 77.44% |
| Checkout-to-Purchase Rate | 45.49% |

### 3. Key Business Insights

- Approximately 20.48% of distinct product viewers
  also triggered an add-to-cart event during the
  reporting period.

- Cart-to-checkout progression was comparatively
  high at 77.44%.

- Checkout-to-purchase progression was 45.49%,
  indicating an area requiring further investigation.

- The difference between checkout users and
  purchasing users was 5,296.

### 4. Business Implications

The checkout stage warrants further investigation
to identify potential barriers to transaction
completion.

Areas for further analysis include:

1. Payment method preferences and failures.
2. Delivery costs and unexpected charges.
3. Checkout experience across devices.
4. Product availability and transaction outcomes.

### 5. Methodological Limitations

These metrics represent distinct users who
triggered each event during the selected
reporting period.

They do not establish that the same users
progressed through all funnel stages in
chronological order.

Users may have triggered events in different
sessions or at different points in time.

Therefore, these figures should be interpreted
as preliminary period-level conversion ratios,
not definitive sequential funnel conversion rates.

A session-level, chronologically ordered funnel
analysis will be conducted to validate the
customer journey.

### 6. Analytical Next Steps

- Develop a session-level conversion funnel.
- Investigate checkout abandonment.
- Analyse purchase and transaction data.
- Identify product-level revenue patterns.
- Evaluate customer retention and repeat purchases.
